## Strengths

- Clear, friendly voice.
- Strong local/Philadelphia angle.
- Good proof points (grants list).

## Weaknesses / redesign opportunities

- Heavy Squarespace template feel.
- No real service detail on the Services page.
- Testimonials are placeholders.
- Grant descriptions are placeholders.
- About page is one long block of text.
- No clear visual hierarchy between pages.
- Missing a strong first impression beyond the hero.
